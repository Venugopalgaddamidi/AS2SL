About This App

This application is designed to help people convert audio speech into sign language animations. It provides a simple interface to type or speak sentences, identifies the key words, and plays corresponding sign language videos for each word.

The goal of this app is to facilitate communication between hearing individuals and those who are speech or hearing-impaired, making interactions more inclusive and accessible.

Users can also explore features like speech-to-text conversion, keyword highlighting, and real-time animation playback, all designed to provide an engaging learning experience.
